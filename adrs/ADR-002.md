## Context
Need transactional ACID integrity while indexing high-dimensional embeddings for AI features.

## Decision
Consolidate on PostgreSQL with the pgvector extension instead of running separate relational and vector DBs.

## Consequences

### Positive
- Unified backup & ACID compliance
- Lower operational footprint

### Negative
- High memory utilization for large vector indexes
