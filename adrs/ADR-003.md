## Context
Early prototype needed rapid semantic search before self-hosted infra was ready.

## Decision
Use managed Pinecone for initial vector lookups.

## Consequences

### Positive
- Zero operational setup
- Fast initial delivery

### Negative
- High SaaS cost scaling
- Siloed data store
