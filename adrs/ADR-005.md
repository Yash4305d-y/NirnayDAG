## Context
Vector search alone misses multi-hop structural causality between engineering choices.

## Decision
Layer a Directed Acyclic Graph traversal engine over pgvector embeddings for hybrid search.

## Consequences

### Positive
- Deterministic dependency tracing
- Zero hallucinations on blast radius queries

### Negative
- Increased query latency across deep DAG paths
